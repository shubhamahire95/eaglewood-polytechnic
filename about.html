Who we are

Transforming aspirations into achievements

Venkateshwara Manav Vikas Mandal's Eaglewood Polytechnic Institute is a technical education institution at Sunanda Nagar, Phule Pimpalgaon, Majalgaon, District Beed 431131.

The institute is approved by AICTE, DTE and the Government of Maharashtra, and affiliated to MSBTE and Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU). Its diploma and degree programs combine academic fundamentals with practical training, industrial exposure, projects and mentoring.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What programmes does Eaglewood offer?

Eaglewood offers diploma and degree engineering programmes in Civil, Computer, Electrical, Civil, Computer, Electrical and AI & Machine Learning through MSBTE and DBATU affiliations.

How do I apply for admission?

Contact the admission office with your academic documents. Our team will guide you on eligibility, CAP allotment, fees and scholarship options. Call +91 94237 16230.

Are hostel and transport facilities available?

Yes. Separate hostel facilities for boys and girls are available along with institute transport routes connecting nearby towns and villages.

What scholarships are available?

Eligible students can apply for government scholarships, EWS benefits, category concessions and institute-supported fee benefits as per current rules.
