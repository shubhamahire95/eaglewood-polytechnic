Our story & management

Engineering education with local roots

Venkateshwara Manav Vikas Mandal’s Eaglewood Polytechnic Institute serves students at Sunanda Nagar, Phule Pimpalgaon, Majalgaon, District Beed 431131.

Approved by AICTE, DTE and the Government of Maharashtra, and affiliated with MSBTE and Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), the institute combines academic foundations with practical training, projects, industrial exposure and faculty mentoring.

Students can explore diploma and degree pathways across Civil, Computer, Electrical, Artificial Intelligence and AI & Machine Learning programs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What programmes does Eaglewood offer?

Eaglewood offers diploma and degree pathways in Civil, Computer, Electrical, Artificial Intelligence and AI & Machine Learning through its MSBTE and DBATU affiliations.

How do I apply for admission?

Apply through the official Maharashtra CAP process for your course. The college team can guide you on eligibility, course options and required documents. This website does not submit an admission application. Call +91 94237 16230 for guidance.

Are hostel and transport facilities available?

Yes. Separate hostel facilities for boys and girls are available along with institute transport routes connecting nearby towns and villages.

What scholarships are available?

Eligible students can apply for government scholarships, EWS benefits, category concessions and institute-supported fee benefits as per current rules.
