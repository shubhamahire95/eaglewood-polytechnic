Your route to admission

How Maharashtra CAP works

Follow the current brochure and schedule from the competent authority for your course. These are the usual stages; dates, eligibility, verification and seat acceptance rules can change by admission year.

  1. 01

    Choose your pathway

    Confirm first-year diploma, direct second-year diploma, first-year engineering or direct second-year engineering eligibility.

  2. 02

    Register on the official portal

    Complete the government application within the published schedule and follow its instructions for document scrutiny.

  3. 03

    Check merit and fill choices

    Review your merit status, then enter and confirm institute and course preferences in the CAP option form.

  4. 04

    Review your allotment

    Check each CAP round and follow the official seat-acceptance steps if you receive an allotment.

  5. 05

    Report to the institute

    After allotment, report to the assigned institute with the required originals and complete formalities by the stated deadline.

The competent authority’s latest information brochure, notices and schedule always take precedence.
